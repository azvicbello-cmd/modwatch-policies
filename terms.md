# ModWatch terms of use

Public product name: **ModWatch**. Approved operator identity: **Victor Bello**. Hosted draft: https://azvicbello-cmd.github.io/modwatch-policies/terms.html. Effective date: **pending operational verification**.

Pre-release draft awaiting operational verification. Not effective yet. The user confirmed Victor Bello as the final approved operator identity. The user's GitHub account will host the separate `modwatch-policies` repository; it need not belong to the production Reddit account owner. A dated, approved copy is required before release.

ModWatch is a community configuration-history tool for current Reddit moderators. Use it only in communities you are authorized to moderate, in accordance with Reddit's terms, rules, and Moderator Code of Conduct. ModWatch is independently operated and is not endorsed by Reddit.

Installation requires the permissions Reddit specifies. Choose the monitored categories and history-retention setting responsibly. Do not place unnecessary personal information in rules, removal reasons, or flair templates, and do not use the app to harass, profile, or surveil people. Do not attempt to bypass moderator access controls.

ModWatch observes configuration; it does not edit rules, remove content, assign flair, or send messages. Snapshot history can miss or combine rapid changes. Trigger metadata does not prove authorship. Retention/storage limits discard older history, and uninstalling or loss of installation storage can remove baselines and history. Keep independent records when needed; ModWatch is not a permanent backup or complete audit log.

Review the approved privacy policy for stored data and request handling. [Contact ModWatch support](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=Aggravating_Slice533&subject=ModWatch%20Support) for issues, feedback, or privacy requests. Do not send credentials or unrelated private data.

The operator may update or discontinue the app and will communicate material changes through its approved listing/documentation and available Reddit channels. Availability and uninterrupted operation are not guaranteed. These terms do not limit rights or obligations that applicable law or Reddit's terms require. Reddit may restrict or remove the app under its platform rules.
